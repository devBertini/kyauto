# KyAuto

Landing, support and legal pages for **KyAuto**, an iOS app that tracks a vehicle's fuel, costs and maintenance, with reminders by date and mileage and a PDF history to share when you sell the car.

- Works 100% offline, with no account.
- App Store privacy label: **Data Not Collected**.
- [Download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/br/app/kyauto/id6778464204)
- Site, support, privacy and terms: https://devbertini.github.io/kyauto/

The pages are plain HTML served by GitHub Pages from this repository.

Made by Claudio Bertini ([devBertini](https://github.com/devBertini)).
